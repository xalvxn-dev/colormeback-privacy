Privacy Policy: Color Me Back

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Color Me Back is made by xalvxnLab ("we").

Offline play

Playing offline collects nothing. Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, best times) and your settings are saved only on your device. You can erase your progress at any time in Settings > Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the game.

Online play, chat and the leaderboard

These features are optional. When you use Play Online or open the Leaderboard, or when the game uploads your best total time, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (provided by Unity Technologies):

An anonymous player ID (no email or account needed)To recognize you between gamesUnity Authentication

  • Chat starts OFF in every room. You choose whether to turn text or voice chat on.
  • You can mute, block and report any player. Rude words in names and messages are hidden.
  • We do not sell your data or show ads.
  • We don't keep chat or voice recordings ourselves. Unity processes them to deliver them; see Unity's policy below.

Unity

The game is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity Gaming Services. Unity's privacy policy explains what Unity collects and how:
https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Children

Online chat with other players is optional and starts off. Parents can keep chat turned off, and offline play never connects to the internet.

Delete your Color Me Back data

You can ask xalvxnLab to delete the online data Color Me Back keeps about you:

  1. Open the game and note your player name (shown in Play Online and on the leaderboard).
  2. Email xalvxn@gmail.com with the subject "Color Me Back data deletion" and include your player name.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your anonymous player ID, your player name, and your Easy and Hard leaderboard times.

What is kept: nothing. Chat messages and voice are never stored by us. Progress saved on your device (levels, stars, settings) is not online; erase it in Settings > Reset all progress or by uninstalling the game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted where the game is available, with a new "Last updated" date.
